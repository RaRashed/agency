Before you apply

What you will need to bring

Requirements vary by embassy and by your personal profile, but almost every file starts with these. Send us photos of what you have on WhatsApp and we will tell you exactly what is missing.

  • Passport valid for at least six months with two blank pages
  • Recent photographs to the destination's exact specification
  • Six months of personal bank statements with a bank solvency letter
  • Employment proof — NOC, salary certificate, or trade licence
  • Confirmed flight itinerary and hotel booking
  • Travel medical insurance for the full duration
  • Marriage or birth certificates where a family relationship is being claimed
  • Any previous visas, refusals or travel history
