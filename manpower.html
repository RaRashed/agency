A recruitment agency that grades workers honestly.

The most expensive thing in overseas recruitment is a worker who arrives unable to do the trade on his contract. The employer loses months, the worker loses everything he borrowed to get there, and both blame the agency — usually correctly.

We built our own trade testing and orientation centre in 2019 for exactly that reason. Every candidate is tested before he is offered to you, the test is recorded, and we grade against the destination's standard rather than against what would be convenient for us.

For employers

How we staff your project

From the demand letter to the arrival confirmation, six stages — with a named account manager and a written timeline you can hold us to.

Demand submitted

You send the demand letter, power of attorney and job order specifying trades, quantity, salary and contract terms.

Attestation

We arrange embassy and ministry attestation of your documents, and register the demand with the emigration authority.

Sourcing & trade testing

Candidates are sourced from our database and district network, then trade-tested at our own centre — video shared with you, or attend in person.

Selection

You select from shortlisted candidates by video interview or an on-site selection trip we host and coordinate.

Documentation

Medical, police clearance, passport processing, visa stamping, insurance and emigration clearance completed by our documentation desk.

Mobilisation

Pre-departure orientation, ticket issuance and departure on the agreed date, with arrival confirmed back to you.

Need workers? Send your demand.

Tell us the trades, the quantity, the salary and the mobilisation date. We will confirm within two working days whether we can meet it — and if we cannot, we will say so rather than waste your quarter.

For job seekers

Your route abroad, without being cheated

Six steps. Every cost written down before you owe it. Registering with us is free — and always will be.

Register free

Come to our office or send your details on WhatsApp. Registering with us never costs money — if anyone asks you to pay to register, walk away.

Skill assessment

We test your trade at our centre and grade you honestly. Being graded correctly is what stops you being sent back at the destination.

Job matching

When a matching demand comes in, we call you with the country, employer, salary, contract length and the full cost breakdown — in writing.

Documents & medical

We guide you through passport, medical, police clearance and attestation, and tell you every fee before it is due.

Training & briefing

Pre-departure orientation covers your contract, your rights, the country's rules, and what to do if something goes wrong.

Fly with support

Ticket issued, airport assistance arranged, and our number stays with you after you land. We follow up in your first month.

Protect yourself — from us as much as anyone

These are our own rules, published so you can hold us to them. If any person claiming to represent this agency breaks one of them, call the office number directly and report it.

  • We never take money in a personal bank account or through mobile money to an individual.
  • Registration with our agency is always free. Nobody at this office may charge you to be added to our database.
  • Every fee you pay comes with an official money receipt showing what it was for.
  • We do not send anyone on a visit or tourist visa to work. That is illegal and it is how people end up stranded.
  • You will see your employment contract, in writing, before you pay the final cost.
