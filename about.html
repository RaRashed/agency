About our agency

Eighteen years of moving people across borders — for a holiday, for a pilgrimage, or for the job that changes their family's future.

18years of licensed operation
Our story

We started because too many people were being cheated.

In 2008 our founder watched a neighbour sell a plot of land to pay an unlicensed broker for a job in Saudi Arabia that did not exist. That is still the most common story in this industry, and it is the reason Skyline Bridge exists.

We built the agency around three rules that have not changed since: work only through legal channels, write down every fee before taking money, and give every client one consultant who is personally answerable for the outcome. It is a slower way to grow. It is also why most of our business now comes from people our old clients sent us.

Today we run four divisions — air ticketing, visa processing, holiday and pilgrimage packages, and overseas manpower supply — from our Dhaka head office with liaison teams in the Gulf.

What we stand for

Four rules we do not bend

These are written on the wall of our office, and they decide which work we accept.

Legality first

If a case cannot be done through the legal channel, we say no. We have turned away work rather than put a client on an irregular route.

One written price

Government fees, service charge, ticket, medical — itemised in a signed quotation before any money changes hands.

One named consultant

Your file is not passed around. The person who takes your enquiry sees it through to arrival and stays on WhatsApp afterwards.

Duty of care

We brief every worker on contract terms, wages and rights before departure, and we follow up after they land.

Milestones

How the agency grew

2008

Founded in Dhaka

Opened as a two-desk ticketing agency with a single IATA consolidator relationship.

2012

Recruitment licence granted

Received the government recruiting agency licence and mobilised our first 120 workers to the UAE.

2016

Gulf offices

Liaison offices opened in Dubai and Doha, giving us direct employer relationships instead of sub-agents.

2019

In-house trade testing

Built our own testing and orientation centre so employers stopped receiving mis-graded workers.

2023

Umrah approval

Approved as a Hajj and Umrah operator; first 400 pilgrims travelled that season.

2026

Where we are now

Four divisions, 60 staff, and more than 31,000 clients served across 32 countries.
