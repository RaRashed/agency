Pricing

You will always know the full cost before you pay anything.

Every quotation we issue separates the three things people usually get confused about: what the government or embassy charges, what the airline or hotel charges, and what we charge. If a cost changes after you have signed, we absorb it — that is the point of putting it in writing.

  • Itemised written quotation, sent on WhatsApp or email.
  • Official money receipt for every payment, no exceptions.
  • Refund terms stated on the quotation, not buried in fine print.
  • No payment ever collected in a personal account.
