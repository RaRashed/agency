What the price covers

Included in every package

If it appears on your itinerary, it is already paid for. We do not sell a low headline price and then charge for the transfers.

Return air ticket Confirmed economy seats on a scheduled carrier, with taxes and one checked bag.
Hotels Twin-sharing rooms with breakfast at the star rating quoted — the actual property named in your voucher.
All transfers Airport pick-up and drop-off plus every transfer inside the itinerary, in air-conditioned vehicles.
Guided sightseeing Entry tickets and a licensed guide at every site listed. Nothing on the itinerary is an optional extra.
Visa processing Application preparation, submission and follow-up for the destination country's tourist visa.
Travel insurance Medical and trip cover for the full duration, with documents accepted by embassies.
Not included

Costs you should budget separately

  • Personal expenses, tipping and laundry
  • Meals not listed in the day-by-day itinerary
  • Optional activities marked as extras
  • Any increase in government taxes after booking
  • Passport issuance or renewal fees
  • Costs arising from flight delays outside our control

Anything you are unsure about, ask before you book — we will answer plainly.
